# StashFlow v1.36.0

## 🔤 Fonts and Appearance

- Choose a font in Appearance settings: System default, Serif, Monospace, Manrope, Outfit, Space Grotesk, Inter, Lora, or JetBrains Mono. The choice applies immediately throughout the app, persists across launches, and is included in configuration backups.
- Scene, gallery, and performer card titles now follow the global UI scale without becoming oversized.
- Refreshed panels, headers, and floating controls with consistent spacing, quieter colors, and frosted backgrounds where content scrolls behind them.

## 🎬 Video Playback Controls

- Play and pause now sit at the center of the video, with previous and next scene controls beside them when the active queue supports navigation. Unavailable directions remain visible but disabled, keeping the controls in place.
- Inline and fullscreen players use consistent button styling and shaded top and bottom edges to keep controls readable over video. Seeking and playback options remain along the bottom.
- Set how long player controls stay visible with a 1–10 second slider in Playback settings. The choice applies during playback and is included in configuration backups; paused controls remain visible.
- Inactive inline players now show the scene screenshot behind the Play button.

## ⭐ Ratings and Details

- A compact star button opens a consistent rating editor with fractional ratings, Clear, and Apply/Cancel actions. Fullscreen image rating keeps its image/gallery selector.
- Scene actions share a horizontal row that scrolls on smaller screens. Desktop layouts show Add Marker, Edit, Refresh, and Delete directly; compact layouts keep them in More.
- Performer and studio details group Favorite, Rating, and Edit below the name. Detail headers put the entity name in the content instead of repeating it in the app bar.
- Gallery details keep rating available in both expanded and collapsed headers. Long-press and More actions on gallery cards open the same metadata panel.

## 🖼️ Image Navigation

- Keyboard image navigation responds faster to arrow keys and advances reliably through rapid presses and held keys. Direction changes, Home/End, touch swipes, and slideshow playback stay in sync, with support for reduced motion.

## 📋 Browsing and Overlays

- Floating list actions and filter panels stay clear of the mini-player, with enough bottom spacing to reach the last items.

## ⚙️ Settings Layout

- The Settings hub shows two columns of action tiles when there is enough room and switches to one column on compact layouts or at larger UI scales.
- App language selection has moved from Interface to Appearance settings.

## 🌐 Translations

- Refined Simplified and Traditional Chinese translations for clearer, more consistent terminology.
