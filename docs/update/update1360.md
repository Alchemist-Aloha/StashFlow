# StashFlow v1.36.0

## 🔤 Fonts and Appearance

- Choose a font in Appearance settings: System default, Serif, Monospace, Manrope, Outfit, or Space Grotesk. The choice applies immediately throughout the app, persists across launches, and is included in configuration backups.
- Scene, gallery, and performer card titles now follow the global UI scale without becoming oversized.

## 🎬 Video Playback Controls

- Play and pause now sit at the center of the video, with previous and next scene controls beside them when the active queue supports navigation. Unavailable directions remain visible but disabled, keeping the controls in place.
- Inline and fullscreen players use consistent button styling and shaded top and bottom edges to keep controls readable over video. Seeking and playback options remain along the bottom.

## ⚙️ Settings Layout

- The Settings hub shows two columns of action tiles when there is enough room and switches to one column on compact layouts or at larger UI scales.
